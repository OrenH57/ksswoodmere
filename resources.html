What to Expect

New to Shaare Shalom?

Kehilat Shaare Shalom is an Orthodox Sephardic shul (synagogue) in Woodmere, NY, led by HaRav David Rubinoff, Shlita, with daily minyanim, Shabbat programs, and weekly Torah learning.

Kehilat Shaare Shalom holds Shacharit on weekday and Sunday mornings and Mincha & Arvit every day at 22 Andover Lane, Woodmere. Current times are on the minyan schedule.

Use the rear entrance at 22 Andover Lane in Woodmere.
