Visitor Information

Before Coming

Basic details for members, guests, and people visiting for minyan or Shabbat.

Use the rear entrance at 22 Andover Lane in Woodmere. Give yourself a few extra minutes the first time so you can find the entrance comfortably.

Shabbat times and special announcements are shared weekly by the shul.

Kids program with Avi is usually held Shabbat afternoon at 5:15 PM, with family-friendly programming when available.

Street parking is generally used in the neighborhood. Please be considerate of driveways, signs, and neighbors when arriving for minyan or Shabbat.

Email the shul before visiting so someone can help with entrance details, seating, or other practical needs.
