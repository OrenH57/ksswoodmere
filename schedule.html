Looking for a Minyan Nearby?

Kehilat Shaare Shalom holds Shacharit Monday through Friday and on Sunday morning, and Mincha and Arvit every day, at 22 Andover Lane in Woodmere. The times above follow the current weekly bulletin, and the next minyan is shown at the top of this page.

At 22 Andover Lane, Woodmere, NY 11598, minutes from Cedarhurst, Hewlett, Inwood, and Lawrence. Use the rear entrance.

Yes. Guests and visitors are welcome at every minyan, and there is nothing to sign up for in advance.

Orthodox Sephardic. Tefillah follows Sephardic minhag, and the kehilla is led by HaRav David Rubinoff, Shlita.
